1. Introduction and scope

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Cannon Blitz (Android package name com.cannonblitz.game), published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Cannon Blitz app for Android distributed through Google Play. It does not apply to other apps, websites or services, including Google's services, which have their own privacy policies.

By using the game you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

2. Information the game collects or processes

Game progress stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can continue playing. This includes coins, upgrades, unlocked cannon colors, level results and best scores, endless-mode best score, daily challenge and daily reward status, awards, gameplay statistics (for example, number of runs played and balls destroyed), sound, music, vibration and screen-shake settings, and counters used to space out ads. This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information. There are no user accounts. The game does not request permission to access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Information processed by Google's advertising and consent software

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. When your device is online, these components connect directly to Google and, according to Google's published disclosures, may collect and share with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device;
  • Device and other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance (for example launch time, hang rate and energy use);
  • Your consent choices, where a consent message is shown, so that your choices can be applied to ad requests.

The game itself does not send any additional identifiers or personal information with ad requests.

Game engine

Cannon Blitz is built with the Unity game engine. Unity's analytics, cloud crash reporting, performance reporting and advertising services are turned off in this game. Depending on engine settings, the Unity engine may send limited technical information about the device (such as device model, operating system and graphics hardware) to Unity Technologies when the game starts; any such connection necessarily reveals your IP address to Unity. Unity's handling of such information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

  • Game progress on your device is used only to run the game: to remember your progress, rewards and settings, and to limit how often ads are shown.
  • Information collected by Google's SDKs is used by Google to deliver ads, measure ad performance (analytics), detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have consented or where permitted by law, to personalize ads.
  • Consent choices are used to decide whether and how ads may be requested.

We do not use your information to build profiles, and we do not receive the personal information that Google's SDKs collect. We may see aggregated, non-identifying reports from AdMob (for example, total ad impressions and earnings).

4. Advertising and third-party services

Google AdMob

The game shows banner ads in menus, interstitial ads between some runs (never during gameplay), and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google is a third party that collects information directly from your device under its own terms and privacy policy. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of "PG".

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

When the game starts, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether a privacy or consent message is required in your region, such as in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, or in US states with applicable privacy laws. If a message is required, it is shown before ads are requested, and ads are only requested when the consent framework allows it.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent choices, your region and your device settings. If you do not consent to personalized ads, or if you have turned off ad personalization or deleted your advertising ID, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which are based on context (for example, the app and your general location) rather than your interests.

Google's policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising data
  • Google My Ad Center (ad personalization settings)

Advertising ID controls on Android

On most Android devices you can open Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads; the exact path varies by device and Android version) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After you delete it, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an identifier. On some older devices the option is called Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive or share it ourselves, because the game does not send personal information to us.

However, the information described in section 2 is collected directly from your device by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform SDK, and is used by Google (and, depending on your consent choices and region, by ad partners that Google works with) for the purposes described in section 3. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. If the Unity engine sends technical information as described in section 2, Unity Technologies processes it under Unity's privacy policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, but in practice we hold no personal information about you.

6. Local storage and online services

The developer does not operate any servers for this game. Your progress is not uploaded to us, and there is no cloud save, leaderboard or online multiplayer. The game can be played without an internet connection; an internet connection is only used for ads and consent messages.

Android backup: Android's automatic app data backup is not turned off for this game. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy the game's saved data to a private, Google-managed backup storage area associated with your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game. This backup is handled by Android and Google, not by us. You can manage it in your device's backup settings (for example Settings > Google > Backup; the path varies by device).

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device stays there until you clear the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Cannon Blitz > Storage > Clear storage, wording varies by device) or uninstall the game. Both actions permanently delete your progress from the device. A copy may remain in your Android backup (see section 6) until that backup is replaced or deleted through your Google account or device backup settings.
  • Information collected by Google's SDKs is retained by Google according to Google's own policies. See How Google retains data it collects.
  • Advertising data: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (section 4), manage ad personalization in My Ad Center, and use the tools described in the Google Privacy Policy to make requests about data Google holds.

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, we have nothing to delete on our side. If you have a question about this, contact us using the details in section 12.

8. Security

According to Google's published disclosures, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using the Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocol. The game does not send data to the developer, so no data is transmitted to us.

Game progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. This saved data is not separately encrypted by the game; anyone with full access to your unlocked device may be able to access it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Cannon Blitz is a casual arcade game. The game does not ask users for their age and is not designed to collect personal information from children: it has no accounts, chat or sharing features, and it does not ask for names, contact details or photos. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of "PG". As described above, Google's advertising software may collect device identifiers and other information from any user of the game.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us (section 12). Because the game does not send information to us, we will explain how to clear the game's data and how to use Google's and Android's controls. If we change the game's intended audience to include children, we will update this policy and the game's advertising settings before that change takes effect.

10. Your privacy choices and consent

  • Consent message: if you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or another region where a consent or privacy message is required, the game shows Google's consent message before requesting ads.
  • Change your choices: when a privacy options entry point is required for your region, an Ad privacy options button appears in the game's Settings screen. Use it at any time to review or change your choices.
  • Android ad controls: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (section 4).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch them.
  • Stop all collection: you can play offline, or uninstall the game, which stops all data collection by the game and the SDKs it contains.
  • Your legal rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, or to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. For data held by Google, exercise these rights with Google. For anything else, contact us (section 12).
